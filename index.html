About

Builder.
Leader.
Learner.

Most recently, I was a Staff Product Manager at GoodLeap, where I led the product strategy for a 150+ MW Virtual Power Plant (VPP) aggregating 20,000+ home batteries across the United States — at the intersection of energy markets, grid technology, and consumer software.

I've built energy products across GoodLeap, Fluence Energy, GM Energy/General Motors, NRG, and Entergy — from incubating new businesses to scaling distributed energy platforms to enabling recurring revenue from $10B+ in projects financed.

I've worked on VPPs since 2018, across device types including:

  • 500 kW Natural Gas Generators at Entergy
  • Smart Thermostat demand response at NRG
  • Electric Vehicle managed charging at GM Energy
  • Utility-Scale BESS global deployments at Fluence Energy
  • Residential batteries and expanding DER types at GoodLeap

I hold an MSc in Innovation & Entrepreneurship from HEC Paris and a BS in Economics from Texas A&M University. I'm a proud Clean Energy Leadership Institute (CELI) Fellow.

Outside the office: Marathoner, Father, Book Collector & Reader of 50+ books a year for the last decade, and a tinkerer across AI tools and side projects.
